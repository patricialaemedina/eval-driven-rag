# Incident response

Critical incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. The incident commander posts an update every 30 minutes until service is restored. A blameless review is due within five business days.

# Customer refunds

Support agents may approve refunds up to $100. Refunds above $100 require approval from a support manager. Approved refunds usually appear within five to ten business days.

# Data retention

Application logs are retained for 30 days. Security audit logs are retained for one year. Customers may request deletion of exported support transcripts.

# Account cancellation

Customers may cancel at any time. Monthly subscriptions remain active until the end of the current billing period. Annual subscriptions are reviewed individually for prorated refunds.

# Travel-agent reimbursements

Travel agents cannot approve reimbursements.
